J.A.R.V.I.S.

JARVIS is a private, personal assistant that one person runs for himself on his own computers. It is not a public service, it has no other users, and nothing can be signed up for here.

It may read its owner's own Google Calendar and Gmail, and save backups to his own Google Drive, only to help him with his day.
