JARVIS privacy policy

JARVIS is a personal assistant used by a single person, its owner, on his own devices. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • The owner's Google Calendar: to tell him about his events and to add or remove events he asks for.
  • The owner's Gmail: to tell him about new mail, find messages he asks about and prepare drafts. Mail is sent only when he confirms it.
  • The owner's Google Drive: only the files JARVIS creates itself, for daily backups of its own memory.

Where the data goes

The data stays on the owner's own server and devices. It is not sold, shared or given to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising. Parts of it may be processed by the AI model the owner uses to answer his questions.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
